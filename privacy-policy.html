This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the TypeBloom website.

Cookies

TypeBloom may use local browser storage to remember your practice progress, personal bests, last selected level, and theme preference. Third-party services such as Google AdSense, when enabled, may also use cookies or similar technologies to serve advertisements based on prior visits to this website or other websites.

Third-Party Services

Google advertising services may be loaded on selected content pages. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, limit, measure, or personalise ads, subject to their own policies and the choices available to you. TypeBloom does not control information processed by those third-party services. For details about Google advertising technologies and controls, review Google’s own privacy and advertising information.

Local Storage

TypeBloom stores practice information in local browser storage so the same browser can remember completed levels, personal-best results, daily challenge history, session summaries, and theme preference. The practice tool does not require a TypeBloom account. Session summaries may include the test type, duration, speed, accuracy, correct-character count, mismatch count, and the most common error patterns. The site does not need to store the full text you typed to display your progress dashboard.

Local browser data is not a cloud backup. You can remove it by clearing this site’s stored data in your browser settings. Doing so may reset your saved progress and preferences.

Children’s Information

This website does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can review the request.
