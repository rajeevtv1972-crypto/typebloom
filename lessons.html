How to use the finger guide

Rest the fingers of your left hand on A, S, D and F, and the fingers of your right hand on J, K, L and semicolon. The small raised marks on F and J help you find the starting position without looking. Use each finger for its nearby group of keys, then return to the home row.

Give yourself time to learn the movement before trying to type quickly. If your wrists or hands become uncomfortable, pause, relax, and adjust your position. A comfortable, repeatable technique matters more than finishing a lesson quickly.

When the basic patterns feel familiar, move to progressive typing practice and try a timed test after a few focused sessions.
