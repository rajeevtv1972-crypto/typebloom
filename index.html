Typing practice guide

Build typing speed with accuracy first

TypeBloom is designed for deliberate, repeatable typing practice. Instead of treating every session as a race, the exercises give you a simple progression from short beginner passages to longer intermediate and advanced challenges. Your results are calculated in the browser, while your practice history and personal bests can remain on your device.

What WPM and CPM mean

WPM (words per minute) is a common way to describe typing speed. It is generally calculated from correctly typed characters using a five-character word standard. CPM (characters per minute) measures correctly typed characters directly. Accuracy matters for both measures because repeated mistakes slow down real-world typing even when a raw speed number looks high.

A simple practice routine

  1. Start with a level that feels comfortable rather than immediately choosing the hardest challenge.
  2. Keep your hands relaxed and look slightly ahead in the passage instead of chasing each character.
  3. Prioritize accurate keystrokes and a steady rhythm; speed can improve naturally as the movements become familiar.
  4. Use timed tests occasionally to measure progress, then return to focused practice.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. TypeBloom works in the browser and can store practice progress locally, so an account is not required for the core typing exercises.

How long should I practice?

Short, consistent sessions can be easier to repeat than occasional long sessions. TypeBloom also includes timed tests from 2 to 15 minutes so you can choose a duration that fits your routine.

Does TypeBloom track my typing history?

The site can save completed levels, personal-best results, the last selected level, and your theme preference in local browser storage. This information is used to keep the practice experience consistent on your device.
