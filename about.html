What is TypeBloom?

TypeBloom is a friendly, no-install browser typing practice tool designed to make improvement feel simple, calm, and useful.

Instead of turning typing into a stressful race, TypeBloom gives you a clear practice path with progressive levels, timed WPM and CPM sessions, and personal-best tracking stored locally in your browser.

What We Offer

  • Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced practice levels
  • Timed Speed Tests with WPM tracking
  • Timed CPM Tests using longer practice passages
  • 2–15 minute test durations
  • Light and dark themes
  • Local progress and personal-best tracking

Our Philosophy

We believe good practice should be accessible without being overwhelming. Accuracy, consistency, and comfortable technique come before simply chasing a bigger number.

Accuracy. Consistency. Confidence. Small improvements add up.

Why We Exist

Typing is a skill used in study, work, communication, and everyday life. TypeBloom exists to provide a focused place where anyone can practice at their own pace without needing an account.

Looking Ahead

TypeBloom can continue to grow with more exercises, useful practice modes, and thoughtful improvements while keeping the experience lightweight and easy to use.
