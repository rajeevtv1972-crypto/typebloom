What is TypeBloom?

TypeBloom is a free, browser-based typing practice tool for people who want to become more comfortable and consistent at the keyboard. It is built around a simple idea: useful practice should give you a clear exercise, understandable feedback, and a way to notice progress over time.

The practice area includes progressive passages, timed WPM and CPM tests, a daily challenge, and short workouts based on common typing mistakes. The intention is to make it easier to practise one skill at a time instead of chasing a speed number without context.

How the practice tools work

Practice levels begin with short passages and move toward longer or more varied text. Timed speed tests focus on words per minute, while CPM tests count correctly matched characters per minute. The Daily Challenge gives you a one-minute exercise with a 95% accuracy goal. After a completed exercise, the result view shows speed, accuracy, correct characters, mismatches, and a comparison with the averages recorded in the current browser.

TypeBloom uses the standard five-character convention when calculating WPM. CPM uses correctly matched characters directly. Accuracy is calculated from the proportion of entered characters that match the target positions in the completed passage. These measurements are useful for tracking your own practice, but different passages and test durations can produce different results.

Progress and privacy

The core typing exercises do not require an account. Completed levels, personal bests, session summaries, and theme preferences are stored in local browser storage so the same browser can remember your practice. This is not a cloud account or a backup service. Clearing site data, changing browsers, or moving to another device can make that local history unavailable.

Some pages may load third-party services such as Google advertising scripts. Those providers may process information according to their own policies and settings. The Privacy Policy explains the local storage and third-party service details.

Our approach to practice

We encourage accuracy before speed, short repeatable sessions, and reviewing mistakes without treating them as failure. A useful next step might be practising a difficult pair of letters, slowing down around punctuation, or taking a longer timed test to see whether your rhythm stays steady.

For practical instruction on WPM, CPM, touch-typing technique, and a ten-minute practice routine, visit the TypeBloom Learning Center. When you are ready to practise, open the typing workspace.

Feedback and questions
